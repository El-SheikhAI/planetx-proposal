1. Executive Summary

PlanetX currently operates on Odoo Community Edition with 72 custom modules. As your Odoo subscription reaches its final year, Nodatx proposes a focused, high-impact migration to ERPNext — the world's leading open-source ERP — covering your core business operations across Accounting, Procurement, Inventory, Sales, and CRM.

This proposal reflects a strategically scoped migration. After thorough technical analysis, we have mutually agreed to exclude the construction and tender management modules from this phase, as these require a level of custom development that is better addressed as a dedicated Phase 2 engagement. Similarly, payroll and attendance engine customization is deferred to allow faster go-live on the core financial and operational modules.

What you get: A fully operational ERPNext system with all your historical accounting data, open transactions, inventory, procurement workflows, and sales operations — delivered in approximately 16 weeks (4 months).

IN SCOPE   Accounting · Procurement · Inventory · Sales · CRM · Security · Reports

DEFERRED   Construction · Tender · Payroll Engine · Attendance Engine · BOM Explosion on SO · Guarantee Letters

2. Scope of Work

This proposal covers the full migration of 45 Odoo modules across six functional areas. The remaining 27 modules are explicitly excluded from this phase and documented for future engagement.

2.1 — Core Deliverables

1. ERPNext System SetupFull ERPNext v16 installation, Frappe framework configuration, production environment with backup strategy, SSL, and email integration.
2. Chart of Accounts MigrationComplete redesign and migration of your Odoo COA to ERPNext root-type hierarchy. All accounts mapped, verified, and balanced.
3. Master Data MigrationCustomers, suppliers, products (with variants and UoM mapping), employees, users, warehouses, price lists, and tax templates.
4. Accounting Core MigrationFull GL/AR/AP migration. All open invoices, open payment entries, unreconciled items. Trial balance validated against Odoo source.
5. Cheque Management SystemPost-dated cheque lifecycle — receive, issue, deposit, clearing, bounce, return. Built on ERPNext marketplace app with custom treasury feed.
6. Procurement & InventoryPurchase requests, POs (dual discount), stock opening balance, inter-warehouse transfers, warehouse access restrictions.
7. Sales OperationsSales orders (total-level discount), per-line delivery location, user-level pricelist filtering, custom invoice print formats.
8. CRM & Access ControlCRM lead management, opportunity tracking, record-level access, field-level permissions, sales team restrictions.
9. Custom Reports & Print FormatsCash flow, budget vs actual, ledgers, payment/receipt vouchers, PO/RFQ formats, invoice templates. All XLSX-exportable.
10. Data Validation & Go-LiveTrial balance reconciliation, AR/AP aging match, stock valuation match, UAT, 50+ user training, parallel run, go-live support.

2.2 — Additional Inclusions

  • Petty cash request → approval → payment → settlement workflow
  • Purchase order dual discount (percentage + fixed amount) computation
  • Sales order multi-step approval workflow
  • Inter-warehouse transfer with transit location and approval
  • 24 custom access rights reconfigured as ERPNext roles
  • XLSX report engine (migrated from Odoo OCA to Frappe xlsxutils)
  • Cash flow dashboard and journal balance kanban views
  • Employee loan management (application, approval, installment, payslip)
  • HR custody tracking (company assets assigned to employees)
  • Employee age auto-calculation and contract end-date notifications
  • All Arabic-language data preserved with proper Unicode handling
